### 完形填空：关于"语言与思维的关系"

The debate over whether language shapes thought or merely expresses pre-existing ideas has fascinated philosophers and linguists for centuries. This question, known as linguistic relativity, suggests that the structure and vocabulary of a language can ___1___ how its speakers perceive and think about the world.

The most famous evidence for this hypothesis comes from studies of color perception. Languages with multiple words for blue perceive shades of blue more ___2___ than languages with a single blue term. This suggests that having a word for something may enhance our ability to ___3___ it.

However, recent research has ___4___ this view. Studies of Russian speakers, who have separate words for light blue (goluboy) and dark blue (siniy), found no ___5___ in blue perception compared to English speakers. Instead, the distinction appears to be ___6___ only when speakers are actively ___7___ about colors.

The relationship between language and thought appears to be more ___8___ than the strong version of linguistic relativity suggests. While language can ___9___ certain cognitive processes, it does not appear to ___10___ our basic ability to perceive and reason about the world.

Cultural context also plays a significant role. In languages where spatial relationships are described using absolute directions (north, south, east, west) rather than relative ones (left, right), speakers show ___11___ spatial memory and navigation abilities. This suggests that language can shape cognitive abilities that extend ___12___ color perception.

The debate continues, but most researchers now ___13___ a middle ground. Language likely serves as a cognitive tool that can enhance certain mental processes without completely determining our ___14___ of reality. The mind appears to be more ___15___ than either extreme position suggests.

The implications of this research extend beyond ___16___ linguistics. Understanding how language affects thought has practical applications in education, cross-cultural communication, and even artificial intelligence development. If language can shape cognition, then learning new languages might ___17___ cognitive abilities in unexpected ways.

Moreover, as AI systems become capable of processing and generating language, questions about the relationship between language and thought take on new ___18___. Can AI systems that master language also develop human-like ways of thinking? Or is the connection between language and thought ___19___ to biological minds?

These questions remain ___20___ , but they highlight the continuing importance of linguistics in understanding the nature of mind and cognition.
