In languages where spatial relationships are described using absolute directions (north, south, east, west) rather than relative ones (left, right), speakers show ___11___ spatial memory and navigation abilities. The debate continues, but most researchers now ___13___ a middle ground. Language likely serves as a cognitive tool that can enhance certain mental processes without completely determining our ___14___ of reality. The mind appears to be more ___15___ than either extreme position suggests.

The implications of this research extend beyond ___16___ linguistics. ## Section III Translation (46题，15分)

### 46. 英译中

**原文：**
The relationship between technology and human connection is more nuanced than often assumed. While digital communication has enabled unprecedented global connectivity, many worry that it comes at the cost of deeper, more meaningful relationships. Research suggests that both fears may be overstated. The impact of technology on social bonds depends largely on how these tools are used rather than the technologies themselves.

**【参考译文】**
技术与人类联系之间的关系比人们通常想象的更加微妙。虽然数字通信实现了前所未有的全球互联，但许多人担心这会以更深、更有意义的关系为代价。研究表明，这两种担忧都可能言过其实。技术对社会纽带的影响很大程度上取决于如何使用这些工具，而不是技术本身。
