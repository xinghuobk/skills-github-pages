## Section III Translation (46题，15分)

### 46. 英译中

**原文：**
The relationship between memory and identity is complex and bidirectional. Our memories shape who we are, influencing our personalities, preferences, and life choices. Conversely, our sense of self affects which memories we encode and how we interpret them. This dynamic interplay suggests that personal identity is not fixed but continuously reconstructed through the lens of experience.

**【参考译文】**
记忆与身份认同之间的关系是复杂的、双向的。我们的记忆塑造了我们是谁，影响着我们的性格、偏好和人生选择。相反，我们的自我认同感影响着我们编码哪些记忆以及如何解读它们。这种动态的相互作用表明，个人身份认同并非固定的，而是通过经验的视角不断重构的。
