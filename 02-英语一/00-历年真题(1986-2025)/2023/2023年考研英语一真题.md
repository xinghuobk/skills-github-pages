The potential applications of this research are ___8___ . Schools considering curriculum changes might benefit from incorporating more music education. For older adults concerned about cognitive decline, musical activities may offer a way to maintain brain health.

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your essay, you should

**【图表主题】** 2020-2023年中国在线教育市场规模变化

2. 疫情后，在线教育成为常态化学习方式

→ 在线教育改变了传统教育模式

The chart illustrates the changes in China's online education market size from 2020 to 2023. As shown in the chart, the market grew from approximately 400 billion yuan in 2020 to about 600 billion yuan in 2023, showing an overall upward trend.

Several factors contributed to this growth. The pandemic in 2020 accelerated the adoption of online learning, as students were unable to attend physical classes. This led to a surge in demand for online education platforms. However, the growth rate slowed after 2021, possibly due to the recovery of offline education and market saturation.

In my view, the online education market will continue to evolve. While it provides convenient access to learning resources, quality assurance remains a key challenge. Platforms should focus on improving content quality and personalized learning experiences to sustain long-term growth.
