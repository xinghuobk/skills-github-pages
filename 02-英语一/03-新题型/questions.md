## 篇章 01：排序题（清洁能源转型）

### 题目说明
下面是一篇关于全球清洁能源转型的文章，其段落顺序已被打乱。请根据文章的逻辑结构，将下列段落（A-E）重新排序。段落的首段已给出。

### 首段（已固定）
The transition from fossil-fuel-based energy systems to renewable and low-carbon alternatives represents one of the most ambitious economic and technological transformations in human history. Over the past two decades, the declining cost of solar panels and wind turbines, combined with growing concern about climate change, has shifted the conversation from whether such a transition is possible to how quickly it can be achieved. Yet the path forward is far from linear, and experts distinguish several distinct phases through which national energy systems typically evolve.

### 待排序段落
**A.** The most recent phase — and, many would argue, the most decisive — is the large-scale deployment of renewable energy at grid parity. Solar photovoltaic modules, which cost roughly 300 dollars per watt in the 1970s, now cost less than 50 cents per watt, making unsubsidized solar power competitive with fossil fuels in most parts of the world. Wind energy has followed a similar trajectory. Hundreds of gigawatts of new renewable capacity have been installed in recent years, and renewables now account for a growing share of electricity generation in dozens of countries.

**B.** Beyond grid-scale electricity generation lies the more complex challenge of decarbonizing sectors that have proven stubbornly resistant to electrification: long-haul transportation, heavy industry, chemical manufacturing, and agriculture. These sectors depend on liquid fuels and high-temperature heat, and achieving meaningful reductions in their carbon footprints will likely require advanced biofuels, green hydrogen, carbon capture and storage, or technologies not yet commercially viable. Many experts believe this phase — if it can be achieved — will define the ultimate success or failure of the global transition.

**C.** The earliest phase of the modern clean energy transition was arguably defined by policy experimentation rather than technological maturity. Governments, particularly in Europe and parts of North America, introduced feed-in tariffs, tax credits, and renewable portfolio standards to create demand for wind and solar power at a time when those technologies were far more expensive than conventional alternatives. These policies served to kick-start manufacturing and drive cost reductions through learning-by-doing, but they were often criticized for being costly and economically inefficient.

**D.** One critical challenge that has moved to center stage is grid-scale energy storage. Renewable energy sources are intermittent — the sun does not always shine, and the wind does not always blow — so matching supply with demand requires new infrastructure. Battery storage, using lithium-ion technology initially developed for consumer electronics and electric vehicles, has emerged as the leading solution. Pumped hydro, compressed-air storage, thermal storage, and emerging technologies such as flow batteries also play and will continue to play important roles.

**E.** As renewables became more affordable, a second phase emerged: the integration of variable renewable energy into existing power grids. The challenge here is not merely adding more wind turbines and solar panels but managing an electricity system that was designed around large, centralized fossil-fuel and nuclear power plants. Grid operators need new tools for forecasting, demand response, and system balancing. Several countries, notably including Denmark, Germany, and Uruguay, have managed to integrate renewable shares of 40 percent or more while maintaining reliable service.

### 正确顺序
**C → E → A → D → B**

### 答案解析
1. **C**：首段末句提到"several distinct phases"，C 段以 "The earliest phase" 开头，明显是最早阶段。
2. **E**：E 段以 "As renewables became more affordable, a second phase emerged" 开头，紧随第一阶段。
3. **A**：A 段描述 "most recent phase ... large-scale deployment of renewable energy at grid parity"，承接第二阶段的技术成熟。
4. **D**：D 段 "One critical challenge that has moved to center stage" 承接大规模部署后出现的储能问题。
5. **B**：B 段开头 "Beyond grid-scale electricity generation" 表明是超越电力部门的后续挑战，末句 "define the ultimate success or failure" 适合作为总结性末段。

### 出处标注
**张剑黄皮书模拟题（新题型精选）**

## 篇章 02：完形填句（心流）

### 题目说明
下面是一篇关于"心流"（flow）心理学概念的文章。请从以下选项（A-F）中选出能填入文章空缺处的最佳句子。选项中有一项为多余项。

The psychological concept of "flow" was first systematically described by Mihály Csíkszentmihályi in the 1970s. Flow refers to a subjective state of complete absorption and effortless concentration in an activity, combined with a loss of self-consciousness and a distorted sense of time. Athletes, musicians, surgeons, writers, and programmers — people in many fields report having experienced the state. **(1)** ________

Csíkszentmihályi and his collaborators identified several conditions that appear to foster flow experiences. The activity, they found, must have clear, immediate goals and provide unambiguous feedback about progress. **(2)** ________ The individual must perceive a balance between the challenges presented by the task and their own perceived skills. When challenges significantly exceed skills, the result is anxiety; when skills significantly exceed challenges, the result is boredom. Flow emerges at the boundary between the two.

The neuroscientific basis of flow is an active area of research. Functional brain imaging studies have shown that flow states are associated with reduced activity in the prefrontal cortex, the region responsible for conscious self-monitoring and deliberate decision-making. **(3)** ________ Other researchers emphasize the role of dopamine pathways, suggesting that the intrinsically rewarding nature of flow experiences may be mediated by the brain's reward system.

The practical implications of flow research are considerable. Many educators have attempted to design curricula and classroom activities that increase the likelihood of flow experiences, with the hope of improving student engagement and learning outcomes. **(4)** ________ Several technology companies have explicitly incorporated flow principles into product design, attempting to create interfaces and games that sustain user engagement for extended periods.

**(5)** ________ Critics argue that flow is inherently difficult to define and measure, and that the conditions described by Csíkszentmihályi are too vague to be reliably operationalized in a research setting. The subjective nature of the experience makes it difficult to study using traditional experimental methods, and some researchers question whether flow is a distinct psychological state at all, or merely a particularly intense form of focused attention. Despite these limitations, the concept continues to exert considerable influence in both academic psychology and popular culture.

### 选项
**A.** This temporary reduction in self-monitoring, researchers believe, may help explain the characteristic loss of self-consciousness and the subjective experience of effortless action.

**B.** Architects, urban planners, and some government agencies have also applied flow principles to the design of public spaces, seeking to create environments that encourage spontaneous interaction and engagement.

**C.** Indeed, it is the deeply pleasurable and intrinsically rewarding character of the flow experience that motivates many people to continue pursuing activities that offer no obvious external reward.

**D.** Some psychologists have suggested that the subjective time distortion reported during flow reflects a temporary suspension of the brain's neural timing mechanisms.

**E.** However, the scientific study of flow is not without its critics and methodological challenges.

**F.** The task must also be of intermediate difficulty — easy enough to be perceived as achievable, but difficult enough to require focused attention.

### 标准答案
**1. C　2. F　3. A　4. B　5. E**

### 答案解析
1. **C**：前文描述许多领域的人都有过心流体验，C 句以 "Indeed" 承接，补充心流体验的愉悦与内在奖励特征，自然过渡到下一段"促进心流的条件"。
2. **F**：前文提到 "clear, immediate goals and ... unambiguous feedback" 是第一个条件，F 句 "The task must also be ..." 以 also 开头，明显是第二个条件。
3. **A**：前文提到 "reduced activity in the prefrontal cortex ... self-monitoring"，A 句 "This temporary reduction in self-monitoring" 直接承接该科学发现。
4. **B**：前文提到 educators，后文提到 technology companies，B 句讲 architects, urban planners 作为中间的第三类应用者，形成连贯排比。
5. **E**：后文整段都是批评者观点，E 句 "However ... not without its critics and methodological challenges" 是标准的承上启下转折句。
6. **D**（多余项）：虽然提到了"时间扭曲"与神经机制，但原文第三段已经讨论了脑成像研究并自然转向多巴胺通路，此处插入 D 会使该段逻辑散乱。

### 出处标注
**考研英语一 2022 年真题新题型（改编）**

## 篇章 03：标题匹配（职场建议）

### 题目说明
下面是一篇包含六个部分的文章，每个部分都是一条职场建议。请从以下标题选项（A-G）中选出最符合每个段落内容的标题。选项中有一项为多余项。

**(1) ________**

One of the most common career mistakes, career coaches agree, is spending too little time reflecting on long-term direction. Many professionals drift from position to position without ever asking what they genuinely value, what kind of work energizes them, or what they hope to achieve. Setting aside time — even a few hours a year — to think carefully about long-term goals can dramatically improve the quality of day-to-day career decisions.

**(2) ________**

Technical skills are essential, but research consistently suggests that so-called soft skills are at least equally important in determining long-term success. Communication, emotional intelligence, negotiation, the ability to work in teams, and the capacity to manage conflict are skills that apply across industries and throughout careers. Unlike technical knowledge, which often becomes obsolete within a decade, these abilities tend to deepen with practice over a lifetime.

**(3) ________**

Mentoring relationships are among the most valuable — and most underrated — resources available to professionals. A good mentor can provide perspective, help identify blind spots, open professional doors, and offer guidance through difficult decisions. Finding a mentor requires not merely asking for advice but building a genuine relationship over time and offering value in return, whether through regular updates on progress, sincere gratitude, or opportunities to help the mentor in return.

**(4) ________**

Almost every important career decision — taking a new job, moving to a new city, starting a company, returning to school — involves uncertainty. Professionals who are paralyzed by the fear of making the wrong decision may find themselves stuck in deeply unsatisfying circumstances. Accepting that perfect information is rarely available, and that reversible decisions are often preferable to no decision at all, is essential to moving forward.

**(5) ________**

The popular ideal of the solitary genius notwithstanding, most significant professional achievements are collaborative. Learning to work effectively with people whose backgrounds, skills, and working styles differ from one's own is not merely a technical skill but also — and perhaps primarily — a matter of attitude. Assuming the best of colleagues, giving credit generously, listening actively, and communicating clearly are the foundation of productive collaboration.

**(6) ________**

Perhaps the most underappreciated career skill is simply consistency over time. People who reliably deliver on their promises, maintain steady effort through periods of frustration, and treat colleagues with consistent respect tend, over the long run, to build reputations that open opportunities no amount of brilliance alone could create. Career success is less frequently the result of one spectacular achievement than the compound interest of thousands of ordinary days done well.

### 标题选项
**A.** Why Consistent, Reliable Performance Matters More Than You Think

**B.** The Importance of Mentoring Relationships in Career Development

**C.** Investing in "Soft Skills" Pays Long-term Dividends

**D.** Why Technical Expertise Is Sufficient for Career Success

**E.** Making Peace With Uncertainty: Deciding Without Perfect Information

**F.** Taking Regular Time to Reflect on Long-term Direction

**G.** Collaboration as a Foundation of Professional Achievement

### 标准答案
**1. F　2. C　3. B　4. E　5. G　6. A**

### 答案解析
1. **F**：首段重点 "spending too little time reflecting on long-term direction"，F 标题完美匹配。
2. **C**：第二段重点 "soft skills are at least equally important"，C 标题匹配。
3. **B**：第三段首句 "Mentoring relationships are among the most valuable"，B 标题直接匹配。
4. **E**：第四段核心词 "uncertainty"、"perfect information is rarely available"，E 标题匹配。
5. **G**：第五段核心 "collaborative"、"productive collaboration"，G 标题匹配。
6. **A**：第六段首句 "consistency over time" 及末段 "compound interest of thousands of ordinary days"，A 标题匹配。
7. **D**（多余项）：与 C 选项观点相反，且与原文对 soft skills 的强调相悖。

## 篇章 04：排序题（记忆的形成与巩固）

### 题目说明
下面是一篇关于记忆形成与巩固的科普文章。请根据逻辑，将段落（A-E）重新排序。首段已固定。

### 首段（已固定）
Human memory is not a single, unitary system but rather a family of related capabilities that involve different brain structures and operate on different timescales. From the fleeting retention of a phone number long enough to dial it, to the permanent storage of a childhood memory, the brain uses distinct mechanisms to encode, store, and retrieve information at different durations. Understanding how these memory systems work — and how they interact — is one of the central projects of cognitive neuroscience.

### 待排序段落
**A.** Consolidation during sleep appears to play a critical role. During deep slow-wave sleep, patterns of neural activity that occurred during waking hours are spontaneously replayed in the hippocampus and cortex, a process believed to transfer memory traces from temporary to permanent storage and to integrate new memories with existing knowledge. REM sleep, by contrast, appears to be particularly important for emotional memory and for the creative integration of seemingly unrelated ideas.

**B.** Short-term memory — sometimes referred to as working memory — involves the temporary retention of small amounts of information for brief periods, typically on the order of seconds to minutes. The classic demonstration of this system is the digit-span test: most people can recall a sequence of roughly seven random digits, plus or minus two, but performance declines sharply beyond that limit. Working memory relies primarily on the prefrontal cortex and related regions.

**C.** The distinction between short-term and long-term memory is supported not only by behavioral experiments but also by striking clinical evidence. Patients with damage to the hippocampus, a brain structure deep within the temporal lobe, can often hold a new phone number in memory for a minute or more but cannot form new long-term memories at all. This condition, known as anterograde amnesia, provides some of the strongest evidence that short-term and long-term memory rely on at least partially distinct brain systems.

**D.** Information destined for long-term storage does not remain static after encoding. Instead, memories undergo a prolonged process of consolidation during which they are gradually transferred from temporary, hippocampus-dependent storage into more stable, distributed cortical representations. This process unfolds over hours, days, and perhaps even years, and it can be disrupted by sleep deprivation, stress, certain drugs, and brain injury.

**E.** Long-term memory itself is not a single system but comprises several distinct forms. Declarative or explicit memory — memory for facts and events that can be consciously recalled — differs from procedural or implicit memory — memory for skills, habits, and conditioned responses that operate largely outside conscious awareness. Riding a bicycle, typing on a keyboard, and recognizing a familiar face all involve procedural memory systems that function differently from the systems responsible for remembering a conversation or a historical date.

### 正确顺序
**B → C → E → D → A**

### 答案解析
1. **B**：首段总述"不同时间尺度的记忆系统"，B 段以 "Short-term memory ... temporary retention ... typically on the order of seconds to minutes" 开头，自然地先介绍最短期的记忆。
2. **C**：C 段 "The distinction between short-term and long-term memory is supported ... by striking clinical evidence" 承接 B 段对短期记忆的介绍，以临床证据为过渡引出长时记忆。
3. **E**：E 段 "Long-term memory itself is not a single system but comprises several distinct forms" 是对 long-term memory 的细分介绍，自然承接 C 段末尾对长时记忆的引入。
4. **D**：D 段 "Information destined for long-term storage does not remain static ... undergoes a prolonged process of consolidation" 承接 E 段对长时记忆类型的介绍，转而讨论巩固过程。
5. **A**：A 段 "Consolidation during sleep appears to play a critical role" 继续细化 consolidation 的机制，以睡眠中的 replay 为例，构成文章的深入分析。

### 出处标注
**考研英语一 2018 年真题新题型（改编）**

## 篇章 05：完形填句（抗生素耐药性）

### 题目说明
请从选项（A-F）中选出能填入文章空缺处的最佳句子。选项中有一项为多余项。

The discovery and widespread use of antibiotics represents one of the most important medical advances of the 20th century. Infections that were routinely fatal before the 1940s — pneumonia, tuberculosis, bacterial meningitis, and common childhood diseases — became treatable. **(1)** ________ Yet the effectiveness of these miracle drugs is increasingly threatened by the emergence and spread of antibiotic-resistant bacteria.

Antibiotic resistance is not a modern invention. Bacteria have evolved sophisticated mechanisms for resisting chemical threats from their competitors for billions of years. **(2)** ________ In the brief period since the introduction of antibiotics in clinical medicine, bacteria have evolved resistance to every antibiotic introduced into widespread use, often within a few years of introduction. Some bacterial strains are now resistant to multiple antibiotics, and a few — popularly known as "superbugs" — are resistant to nearly all available treatments.

**(3)** ________ Agricultural use of antibiotics, particularly for growth promotion in livestock, accounts for roughly three-quarters of all antibiotic consumption worldwide and is increasingly recognized as a major contributor to the emergence of resistant bacteria. In healthcare settings, the selective pressure created by widespread antibiotic use, combined with poor infection-control practices, has created ideal conditions for the emergence and spread of resistant pathogens.

The consequences are both medical and economic. Patients infected with resistant bacteria typically experience longer hospital stays, require more expensive second- and third-line treatments, and face significantly higher mortality rates than patients with antibiotic-susceptible infections. **(4)** ________ Several public-health agencies have warned that without urgent action, the world risks entering a "post-antibiotic era" in which common infections once again become life-threatening.

Addressing antibiotic resistance requires action on multiple fronts. Reducing unnecessary antibiotic prescriptions in both human and veterinary medicine is essential. **(5)** ________ International cooperation, including improved surveillance and shared standards for antibiotic use in agriculture, will also be crucial. The development of new antibiotics, despite the scientific and commercial challenges involved, remains an important long-term component of any comprehensive strategy.

### 选项
**A.** What is new, rather, is the unprecedented intensity of selective pressure that the widespread use of antibiotics in medicine and agriculture has created.

**B.** Improving infection control in hospitals and clinics, investing in rapid diagnostic tests, and developing vaccines can also reduce the need for antibiotic use.

**C.** Surgeons could operate with far less risk of postoperative infection, and chemotherapy for cancer — which often suppresses the immune system — became feasible.

**D.** Some economists estimate that antibiotic resistance could cost the global economy trillions of dollars annually by the middle of the century through increased healthcare costs and lost productivity.

**E.** The most important single factor in the spread of antibiotic resistance, however, is the lack of public awareness about the dangers of unnecessary antibiotic use.

**F.** Several factors have contributed to the current crisis.

### 标准答案
**1. C　2. A　3. F　4. D　5. B**

### 答案解析
1. **C**：前文列举了抗生素治疗肺炎、肺结核等疾病的例子，C 句以 "Surgeons could operate ... chemotherapy ... became feasible" 继续说明其医学贡献，随后以 "Yet" 转折引入问题。
2. **A**：前文提到"耐药性并非现代发明，细菌已进化出抵抗机制数十亿年"，A 句 "What is new, rather, is the unprecedented intensity of selective pressure" 自然转折，对比古老机制与现代人为加剧的选择压力。
3. **F**：后文整段讨论农业使用和医疗环境使用两大因素，F 句 "Several factors have contributed to the current crisis" 是标准的主题引入句。
4. **D**：前文讲"医疗与经济后果"，并详细讨论了医疗后果（longer hospital stays, higher mortality），D 句 "Some economists estimate ... trillions of dollars annually" 自然承接经济后果。
5. **B**：前文 "Reducing unnecessary antibiotic prescriptions ... is essential" 提出第一条建议，B 句 "Improving infection control ... investing in rapid diagnostic tests ... developing vaccines can also reduce" 以 also 开头，是第二条建议。
6. **E**（多余项）："最重要的单一因素是公众意识缺乏"，与原文随后列举的具体措施框架不符。

### 出处标注

## 篇章 06：例证匹配（可持续设计创新）

### 题目说明
下面左栏列出了五家公司或机构在可持续设计方面的创新实践（1-5），右栏列出了六项创新成果描述（A-F）。请为每项创新实践匹配最恰当的成果描述。

### 创新实践
**1.** The Dutch architectural firm MVRDV, known for experimental urban projects, has developed a concept for a vertical farm integrated into a residential high-rise building, with each apartment connected to a personal greenhouse.

**2.** Patagonia, the outdoor apparel company, has for decades pursued an unusual business strategy: encouraging customers to repair, reuse, and eventually return products for recycling, and publicly urging consumers to buy less overall.

**3.** The Danish engineering firm BIG collaborated with a waste management company to design a combined waste-to-energy plant and ski slope on the outskirts of Copenhagen, designed to operate as carbon-neutral.

**4.** Interface, a major global manufacturer of commercial carpet tiles, pioneered the concept of "Mission Zero" — a commitment to eliminate any negative environmental impact from its operations by 2020 — and has since worked toward a fully circular business model in which old carpets are collected and recycled into new ones.

**5.** Researchers at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology have developed a process for manufacturing cement that captures carbon dioxide during production, potentially reducing the carbon footprint of one of the world's most carbon-intensive building materials.

### 成果描述
**A.** A company that treats its products not as disposable commodities but as durable goods to be maintained and returned, aligning its business strategy with long-term environmental goals despite the apparent contradiction with maximizing sales volume.

**B.** An academic research project addressing the emissions of an industry responsible for roughly 8 percent of global carbon dioxide emissions, offering the possibility of a fundamentally less carbon-intensive production process.

**C.** A bold architectural project in northern Europe that turns infrastructure conventionally perceived as unpleasant — a waste treatment facility — into a public recreational landmark while also generating electricity.

**D.** A flooring company that was among the first to commit explicitly to eliminating its environmental footprint and, more recently, to designing its production cycle so that its products become the raw materials for future products rather than waste.

**E.** A speculative high-rise design integrating food production into residential architecture, blurring the line between home and farm and potentially reducing the environmental impact of food transportation.

**F.** A government agency that imposes strict environmental regulations on manufacturers, forcing companies to reduce their carbon emissions through mandatory emissions trading schemes and penalties for noncompliance.

### 标准答案
**1. E　2. A　3. C　4. D　5. B**

### 答案解析
1. **E**：MVRDV 设计"垂直农场+公寓+个人温室"，匹配 E 项"将粮食生产融入住宅建筑的高层设计"。
2. **A**：Patagonia 鼓励"修理、复用、回收、少买"，匹配 A 项"将产品视为耐用品而非一次性商品"。
3. **C**：BIG 设计"垃圾焚烧厂+滑雪场"组合，匹配 C 项"将垃圾处理设施变成公共娱乐地标"。
4. **D**：Interface "Mission Zero" 及"旧地毯回收为新地毯的循环模式"，匹配 D 项"首批明确承诺消除环境足迹的地板公司之一，朝循环商业模式迈进"。
5. **B**：MIT 研究"生产过程中捕获二氧化碳的水泥"，匹配 B 项"针对占全球碳排放约 8% 的行业的学术研究项目"。
6. **F**（多余项）：描述的是政府监管机构，原文五个案例都是企业或研究机构，无法匹配。

## 篇章 07：标题匹配（哲学与日常生活）

### 题目说明
下面是一篇包含六个部分的文章，每个部分讨论哲学如何在日常生活中发挥作用。请从标题选项（A-G）中为各段选择最合适的标题。选项中有一项为多余项。

**(1) ________**

Many people encounter philosophy for the first time as an abstract academic subject remote from daily life. Yet philosophical questions — about how to live, what matters, how to treat others, and what to believe — arise spontaneously in almost everyone's experience. A difficult career choice, an ethical dilemma at work, a painful conversation with a family member: these moments force practical reasoning of a genuinely philosophical kind.

**(2) ________**

The ancient philosophical schools — the Stoics, Epicureans, Aristotelians, and Skeptics — did not understand philosophy as a theoretical discipline primarily taught in universities. They understood it, instead, as a way of life. Stoic practices of negative visualization, for example, were designed not to satisfy intellectual curiosity but to cultivate resilience in the face of loss and to reduce anxiety about material possessions.

**(3) ________**

Much of contemporary life is organized around the pursuit of happiness as subjective feeling. Philosophy offers a more complex vocabulary for thinking about well-being, distinguishing not only between short-term pleasure and long-term fulfillment but between different conceptions of what a good human life consists in. These distinctions can help individuals make more thoughtful choices about careers, relationships, and personal goals.

**(4) ________**

Disagreements about public policy — about climate, education, healthcare, or economic inequality — frequently turn on unexamined philosophical assumptions about justice, freedom, equality, and responsibility. Learning to identify and evaluate these underlying assumptions is one of the most practical applications of philosophical training and a prerequisite for genuinely informed civic participation.

**(5) ________**

The digital age has created new forms of experience — constant connectivity, social media, algorithmic filtering of information — that raise questions with deep philosophical roots: questions about the nature of attention, the conditions of authentic relationship, the meaning of privacy, and the distinction between knowledge and information. Philosophy cannot resolve these questions by itself, but it can provide frameworks for thinking about them more clearly.

**(6) ________**

Perhaps the most direct practical benefit of philosophical training is its emphasis on argumentation and clear reasoning. Learning to identify logical fallacies, to distinguish good arguments from bad ones, and to evaluate evidence critically is useful not only in academic settings but in virtually every professional and personal context. Employers frequently report that they value these abilities more than narrow vocational skills.

### 标题选项
**A.** Philosophical Assumptions Underlying Public Policy Debates

**B.** The Ancient Understanding of Philosophy as a Way of Life

**C.** Re-thinking Well-being Beyond Subjective Happiness

**D.** The Irrelevance of Philosophy in Modern Professional Life

**E.** New Technologies Raise Old Philosophical Questions

**F.** Practical Reasoning and Philosophical Reflection in Everyday Life

**G.** The Practical Value of Training in Argumentation and Critical Reasoning

### 标准答案
**1. F　2. B　3. C　4. A　5. E　6. G**

### 答案解析
1. **F**：首段核心 "philosophical questions ... arise spontaneously in almost everyone's experience" 和 "force practical reasoning"。
2. **B**：第二段 "ancient philosophical schools ... understood it, instead, as a way of life"。
3. **C**：第三段重点 "pursuit of happiness as subjective feeling" 与 "more complex vocabulary for thinking about well-being"。
4. **A**：第四段 "Disagreements about public policy ... frequently turn on unexamined philosophical assumptions"。
5. **E**：第五段 "The digital age has created new forms of experience ... that raise questions with deep philosophical roots"。
6. **G**：第六段 "most direct practical benefit of philosophical training is its emphasis on argumentation and clear reasoning"。
7. **D**（多余项）：与 G 选项和原文观点相反，原文明确强调哲学在职场的相关性。

## 新题型解题技巧总结

### 一、排序题
1. **锁定首段/末段**：首段通常引入话题，末段通常总结。
2. **寻找代词指代**：this, that, these, those, such, it 等词暗示前一段内容。
3. **逻辑关联词**：however, therefore, moreover, meanwhile, as a result 等是解题关键。
4. **时间/数字线索**：earliest phase, second phase, most recent phase 等时间序列词特别重要。
5. **段落首尾勾连**：上段末句话题应与下段首句话题一致。

### 二、完形填句
1. **关注空缺处前后语境**：前句与后句之间的逻辑关系（转折、因果、递进、举例、解释）。
2. **代词线索**：选项中的 this, that, these 必须能在前文找到指代对象。
3. **重复词与同义词**：正确选项常含有与上下文相同或同义的关键词。
4. **语法连贯性**：时态、语态、句式应与上下文保持一致。
5. **多余项特征**：通常是内容上看似相关但逻辑无法嵌入的句子。

### 三、标题匹配
1. **抓段落主题句**：英语段落通常首句即主题句。
2. **关键词同义替换**：正确标题是段落核心词的同义改写。
3. **警惕细节陷阱**：某个细节不应被当成主旨。
4. **多余项**：常是与原文观点相反的标题。
5. **时间管理**：这类题应快速完成（8-10 分钟），给翻译和写作留出时间。

### 备考建议
- 近十年真题新题型务必全部做完两遍
- 注意培养对段落结构和逻辑衔接的敏感度
- 多阅读英文报刊文章，留意段落之间的连接方式
