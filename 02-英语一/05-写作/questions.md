## 写作题 01

### 小作文：建议信
**题目：** 假设你是某大学的学生李明，你认为学校图书馆在某些方面需要改善。请给图书馆馆长写一封约 100 词的建议信，内容包括：
1. 说明写信目的
2. 提出两到三点具体建议
3. 表达感谢与期待

### 参考范文
Dear Director,

I am writing to offer a few modest suggestions for improving our university library, a facility I value deeply.

First, extending opening hours during final-examination weeks would be enormously helpful to students. Second, introducing an online reservation system for study rooms could reduce the daily chaos of early-morning queues. Finally, expanding the English-language collection in the social sciences would better support students preparing for international programs.

I hope you will consider these ideas. Thank you for your dedication to making our library a true center of learning.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 写作题 02

### 大作文：图表作文
**题目：** 下面的图表展示了 2010 年至 2023 年中国某大型城市人均公园绿地面积的变化。请根据图表写一篇约 160-200 词的英语作文，内容包括：
1. 描述图表
2. 分析可能原因
3. 谈谈你的看法

**图表数据：** 2010 年 7.8 ㎡ → 2015 年 10.5 ㎡ → 2020 年 13.1 ㎡ → 2023 年 14.8 ㎡

### 参考范文
The chart reveals a steady and substantial increase in per-capita public green space in a major Chinese city between 2010 and 2023, rising from 7.8 square meters to 14.8 square meters — nearly doubling over thirteen years. The growth was particularly rapid between 2010 and 2015 and has since continued at a more moderate but still encouraging pace.

Several factors account for this trend. On the policy level, the Chinese government has made ecological civilization a national priority, mandating stricter environmental standards and investing heavily in urban green infrastructure. City planners have increasingly recognized green spaces not as decorative luxuries but as essential components of public health, contributing to cleaner air, reduced urban heat-island effects, and improved mental well-being.

In my view, this trend reflects a welcome shift from purely quantity-driven urban development toward a more balanced model that values quality of life. While challenges remain — including equitable access to green space across different neighborhoods — the direction of change is clearly positive. Cities that continue to invest in nature within their borders will reap long-term benefits in public health, social cohesion, and environmental sustainability.

## 写作题 04

### 大作文：议论文
**题目：** 有人认为，随着人工智能等新技术的发展，许多传统技能（如手写、计算、记忆）将逐渐失去其重要性；也有人认为，这些传统技能仍具有不可替代的价值。请写一篇约 180-220 词的英语作文，谈谈你的观点。

### 参考范文
The rise of artificial intelligence and digital tools has sparked an enduring debate about the value of traditional skills such as handwriting, mental arithmetic, and rote memorization. While some argue these abilities are now obsolete in an era of smartphones and AI assistants, I believe they remain fundamentally valuable, though in ways that deserve careful rethinking.

Critics contend that traditional skills waste time better invested in higher-order thinking. Why labor over multiplication when calculators return instant answers? Why practice handwriting in a world of keyboards and voice recognition? From this perspective, schools should concentrate on teaching creativity, critical thinking, and digital literacy rather than cultivating abilities that machines perform far more reliably.

Yet this argument overlooks the deeper purposes that traditional skills serve. Handwriting, research suggests, develops fine motor coordination and engages different cognitive pathways than typing, aiding memory retention and conceptual understanding. Mental arithmetic trains numerical intuition that helps students estimate, reason statistically, and detect errors in AI-generated results. Memorization — though devalued in some educational philosophies — builds the mental library of knowledge on which creative thinking and critical evaluation ultimately depend.

In conclusion, the appropriate response to technology is not to abandon traditional skills but to redefine their purpose. They should be taught as foundations of cognitive development, not as ends in themselves, and selectively combined with new tools. The most capable minds of the future will be those that know both how to use AI and when to trust their own intelligence.

## 写作题 06

### 大作文：图表作文
**题目：** 下面的图表展示了 2000 年、2010 年、2020 年和 2023 年全球一次能源消费结构的变化（煤炭、石油、天然气、可再生能源、核能的百分比）。请写一篇约 180-220 词的英语作文，描述并评论这一变化。

**参考数据：**
- 2000: 煤炭 35%，石油 38%，天然气 21%，可再生 4%，核能 2%
- 2010: 煤炭 33%，石油 34%，天然气 24%，可再生 7%，核能 2%
- 2020: 煤炭 27%，石油 31%，天然气 25%，可再生 14%，核能 3%
- 2023: 煤炭 25%，石油 29%，天然气 23%，可再生 19%，核能 4%

### 参考范文
The chart illustrates the dramatic transformation of the global primary energy mix between 2000 and 2023. Fossil fuels — coal, oil, and natural gas — have together declined from roughly 94 percent of total consumption to approximately 77 percent, while the share of renewable energy sources has nearly quintupled, rising from a mere 4 percent to 19 percent. Nuclear energy has grown more modestly, from 2 percent to 4 percent.

Several interconnected forces drive this transition. First, the rapidly declining cost of solar and wind power has made renewable energy economically competitive with fossil fuels in most parts of the world, attracting massive private investment. Second, growing scientific consensus about climate change, reinforced by the Paris Agreement and subsequent policy frameworks, has pressured governments and corporations to decarbonize. Third, public concern about air pollution and energy security — particularly heightened in regions dependent on imported fossil fuels — has accelerated political commitments to clean energy.

Despite these encouraging trends, the pace of change remains insufficient relative to the scale of the climate challenge. Fossil fuels still supply more than three-quarters of global energy, and absolute demand for coal, oil, and gas continues to rise in many developing economies. Achieving net-zero emissions by mid-century will require not merely incremental growth of renewables but a fundamental restructuring of global energy systems, massive investment in grid infrastructure and energy storage, and international cooperation to ensure that developing nations can participate in the transition without sacrificing economic development. The shift visible in this chart is real and accelerating, but its ultimate outcome depends on choices that policymakers, businesses, and citizens have yet to make.

## 写作题 08

### 大作文：议论文
**题目：** 近年来，短视频平台极大地改变了许多人的阅读习惯。有人认为，短视频碎片化的信息挤压了深度阅读的时间；也有人认为，短视频让知识普及变得更加容易。请写一篇约 180-220 词的英语作文，谈谈你的看法。

### 参考范文
The explosive growth of short-video platforms in recent years has fundamentally reshaped how people consume information. While short-form content has undoubtedly made certain kinds of knowledge more accessible, it also raises serious concerns about declining deep reading and the erosion of sustained attention.

Advocates of short-video learning argue persuasively that these platforms democratize access to knowledge. Complex ideas in science, history, and philosophy are condensed into engaging, visually compelling episodes of three to five minutes, reaching audiences who might never open a textbook. For busy professionals, parents, and students alike, short videos offer an efficient way to learn something new during otherwise wasted minutes. Many educators have embraced the format as a gateway to deeper engagement, using short episodes to spark curiosity before directing viewers to longer-form content.

Nevertheless, the concerns about short videos' cultural impact deserve serious consideration. Cognitive research suggests that shallow, fragmented content trains the brain to expect constant novelty and rapid payoff, making sustained reading — the kind required for genuine understanding of complex subjects — increasingly difficult. When readers consume thousands of disconnected fragments rather than carefully constructed arguments, they risk sacrificing depth for breadth. Moreover, the algorithmic design of short-video platforms, which rewards engagement over educational value, tends to push content toward entertainment rather than insight, often generating superficial understanding masquerading as knowledge.

In my view, short videos are neither inherently good nor inherently harmful — they are tools whose impact depends on how they are used. The wisest approach is not to reject short-form content entirely but to cultivate a balanced information diet: treating short videos as sparks for curiosity rather than substitutes for reading, and deliberately protecting time for books, long-form essays, and other sources of sustained learning. Technology can enhance education, but only when guided by thoughtful individuals who remember that genuine knowledge, unlike entertainment, rarely comes for free.

## 写作题 10

### 大作文：图表作文
**题目：** 下面的图表展示了 2000 年至 2023 年中国高等教育毛入学率的变化。请写一篇约 180-220 词的英语作文，描述图表、分析原因并评论其意义。

**图表数据：** 2000 年 10.5% → 2010 年 26.5% → 2020 年 54.4% → 2023 年 59.6%

### 参考范文
The data reveal an extraordinary expansion of higher education access in China between 2000 and 2023, with the gross enrollment rate rising from just over 10 percent to nearly 60 percent. The pace of change has been particularly striking since 2010, roughly doubling in little more than a decade and transforming higher education from an elite privilege to a mass experience.

Three major forces drive this transformation. First, the Chinese government has systematically expanded university capacity, supporting the construction of new campuses, the hiring of additional faculty, and a nationwide system of student loans and grants designed to ensure that financial barriers do not prevent qualified students from attending university. Second, economic development has dramatically increased the demand for educated workers in knowledge-intensive sectors such as information technology, finance, advanced manufacturing, and research — making higher education both an individual investment and a national strategic priority. Third, rising family incomes have made tuition and living costs more affordable for a growing middle class, while cultural traditions emphasizing education have sustained strong demand.

The implications of this transition are profound. On the positive side, mass higher education has contributed to the remarkable upgrading of China's labor force, supported its technological advancement, and improved social mobility for millions of families whose parents never had the opportunity to attend university. At the same time, rapid expansion raises legitimate concerns. Questions persist about whether educational quality has kept pace with enrollment growth, whether universities are equipping graduates with genuinely relevant skills, and whether the labor market can absorb the growing numbers of graduates without widening inequality between degree-holders and non-degree-holders. Additionally, the intense competition for places at the most prestigious universities has contributed to an educational arms race that imposes heavy burdens on students and families, raising broader questions about the purpose and human cost of mass higher education. In short, while the expansion of higher education stands as a genuine social achievement, the quality, accessibility, and ultimate value of that education remain challenges that policymakers, educators, and society at large must continue to address.

## 写作题 12

### 大作文：议论文
**题目：** 有人认为，环境保护必然会限制经济增长，发展中国家应优先发展经济再考虑环境问题；也有人认为，可持续发展要求我们必须同时追求经济繁荣与环境保护。请写一篇约 180-220 词的英语作文，阐明你的观点。

### 参考范文
The relationship between environmental protection and economic development has been debated for decades, particularly in developing nations where the urgency of lifting people out of poverty can seem to conflict with the long-term demands of sustainability. While the trade-off view has intuitive appeal — and was, in fact, historically dominant in the growth strategies of now-developed nations — I believe that contemporary evidence overwhelmingly supports the position that environmental responsibility and economic prosperity are not merely compatible but actually mutually reinforcing.

The traditional argument — often summarized as "grow first, clean up later" — holds that poor countries must prioritize immediate economic expansion over environmental protection. Proponents point to the historical pattern: Britain, the United States, and other early industrializers polluted heavily during their growth phases and only invested in environmental protection after achieving prosperity. Attempting the reverse, the argument continues, would condemn developing nations to permanent poverty by raising production costs, discouraging investment, and slowing job creation. Environmental standards, from this perspective, are a luxury that only wealthy societies can afford.

Yet this reasoning, however compelling in earlier eras, no longer reflects either the scientific evidence or the economic realities of the 21st century. Environmental degradation imposes severe and growing economic costs: air pollution increases healthcare spending and reduces worker productivity; water scarcity threatens agriculture and industrial production in vast regions of the developing world; climate change, if left unaddressed, poses existential risks to coastal cities and agricultural systems that support billions of people. These costs are not hypothetical or distant — they accumulate daily and disproportionately burden the poor, who are least equipped to adapt.

More fundamentally, the historical pattern itself may no longer be available. The technologies, economic structures, and global environmental conditions facing today's developing nations are profoundly different from those of the 19th and early 20th centuries. Thanks to dramatically declining costs, renewable energy is now often cheaper than coal — meaning developing nations can build modern, clean energy systems without repeating the polluting pathway of their predecessors. Sustainable agriculture, circular-economy principles, and ecotourism offer genuinely viable development strategies that protect the environment while creating jobs and reducing long-term costs. In other words, the assumption that environmental protection necessarily slows growth is increasingly false; in many sectors, the opposite is now true.

None of this denies the genuine difficulties developing nations face. Investing in clean infrastructure, retraining workers, and building effective environmental governance institutions requires resources and political commitment. The international community has a responsibility to support these efforts through technology transfer, climate finance, and knowledge sharing — a responsibility reflected in frameworks such as the Paris Agreement and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. But the direction of travel is clear: sustainable development is not an impossible ideal but a practical necessity, and the nations that embrace it early are likely to enjoy competitive advantages in the green economy of the future. The trade-off between growth and the environment was never as sharp as claimed, and in the 21st century, it is rapidly disappearing.

## 写作题 14

### 大作文：图表作文
**题目：** 下面的图表展示了 2019 年至 2024 年受访上班族对远程办公的接受度变化（满分 10 分）。请写一篇约 180-220 词的英语作文，描述图表、分析原因并评论其影响。

**图表数据：** 2019 年 5.2 分 → 2020 年 6.8 分 → 2022 年 7.5 分 → 2024 年 7.3 分

### 参考范文
The data show a remarkable shift in worker attitudes toward remote work between 2019 and 2024. Before the pandemic, the average acceptance score among surveyed office workers was a modest 5.2 out of 10, reflecting widespread employer skepticism and limited institutional experience with distributed work models. Following the forced experiment of 2020, when lockdowns pushed millions of employees to work from home, the acceptance score jumped dramatically to 6.8, and it has since remained roughly stable in the 7.3-7.5 range — significantly higher than the pre-pandemic baseline.

Several factors explain this dramatic shift. Most importantly, the pandemic demonstrated at scale that remote and hybrid work models could succeed, invalidating the long-held assumption that productivity required physical presence in the office. Employees discovered benefits ranging from eliminated commutes and more flexible schedules to improved work-life balance and better ability to care for family members. Employers, meanwhile, discovered that distributed teams could maintain or even increase productivity in many roles while reducing real estate costs and expanding talent pools beyond geographic limits.

The slightly declining score between 2022 and 2024 — from 7.5 to 7.3 — reflects not a rejection of remote work but rather the recognition that it is not without its challenges. Some employees report feelings of isolation, blurred boundaries between work and personal life, and difficulties with spontaneous collaboration and mentoring, particularly for younger workers early in their careers. Some employers, meanwhile, have worried about team cohesion, organizational culture, and performance measurement in fully virtual environments. These concerns have led many companies to adopt hybrid models — combining scheduled in-office days with remote flexibility — as a middle ground that preserves the benefits of both approaches.

The long-term consequences of this shift are likely to be profound, extending well beyond individual work preferences. The growing acceptance of remote work is reshaping urban geography as demand for office space in central business districts declines while suburban and rural housing markets adjust to new patterns of residency. It is also reshaping family dynamics, gender equity, and the relationship between work and other life priorities. While the optimal balance between remote and in-person work will continue to evolve — and will undoubtedly vary across industries, roles, and individuals — the data make clear that the pre-2020 office-first model is unlikely to return as the default. The pandemic has permanently altered how we work, and the organizations that succeed will be those that design thoughtful, flexible systems that honor both the productivity gains and the human needs revealed by the remote-work experiment.

## 写作题 15

### 大作文：议论文
**题目：** 有人认为，在知识快速更新的时代，终身学习比以往任何时候都更重要；也有人认为，过度强调学习给现代人带来不必要的压力。请写一篇约 180-220 词的英语作文，阐述你的观点。

### 参考范文
The idea of lifelong learning has moved from a cliché of motivational speakers to a genuine necessity of modern life. In an era when knowledge, technology, and industries evolve more rapidly than ever before, the belief that we can "finish" our education after college or graduate school is no longer merely outdated — it is a recipe for personal and professional stagnation. Yet the growing emphasis on perpetual learning also raises legitimate concerns about pressure, burnout, and the risk of turning every human experience into an instrument of self-improvement.

The case for lifelong learning is grounded in the changing nature of work and society. Automation and artificial intelligence are reshaping entire occupations at an accelerating pace. A young person entering the workforce today can reasonably expect to change jobs, industries, and careers multiple times over a working life that may extend six decades or more. Technical skills that were cutting edge five years ago may now be obsolete; entire industries that did not exist a generation ago now employ millions. Beyond career necessity, lifelong learning enriches personal life: it keeps the mind active in older age, deepens our engagement with the world, and opens doors to new communities and experiences. Universities, online platforms, libraries, and community organizations now offer extraordinary learning opportunities that previous generations could scarcely imagine.

Yet enthusiasm for learning must not become a source of unnecessary pressure or guilt. The expectation that every free moment should be "productive" — every commute a podcast, every evening a course, every weekend a workshop — risks turning human life into an endless exercise in optimization. People need rest, recreation, unstructured time, and experiences undertaken for their own sake, not because they contribute to professional advancement or personal "growth." The pressure to be perpetually learning can be particularly intense for young professionals and students, who already face fierce competition, financial stress, and uncertainty about their futures. We must defend the legitimacy of doing nothing useful, of reading books without a practical goal, and of spending time in unproductively joyful conversation with friends.

In my view, the healthiest attitude lies somewhere between complacency and obsession. Lifelong learning should be understood not as a burdensome duty but as an invitation to remain curious — to follow our interests, to ask questions, and to remain open to new ideas throughout life. The best motivation for learning is not fear of obsolescence, though that fear may be well-founded; it is genuine curiosity and a love of understanding. Learning for its own sake — whether through formal programs, reading, conversation, or direct experience — nourishes the mind and helps people adapt to change without sacrificing their peace of mind. In short, we need learning cultures that encourage growth without punishing imperfection, and that value the full human being over the endlessly productive one.

## 写作备考技巧总结

### 一、写作评分标准（考研英语一）
1. **内容完整性**（约 20%）：是否覆盖题目要求的所有要点。
2. **语言准确性**（约 40%）：语法、拼写、标点、用词的正确性。
3. **篇章连贯性**（约 20%）：段落组织、逻辑连接、过渡自然。
4. **语言丰富度**（约 20%）：词汇与句式的多样性。

### 二、小作文备考策略
1. **熟悉书信格式**：开头问候、结尾敬语、署名。
2. **掌握常见类型**：建议信、邀请信、投诉信、道歉信、推荐信、请求信、通知。
3. **积累固定表达**：
   - 开头：I am writing to express/request/inform ...
   - 正文：First, ... Second, ... Finally, ...
   - 结尾：I would greatly appreciate ... / Thank you for your consideration.
4. **控制长度**：小作文约 100 词，过长不会加分。

### 三、大作文备考策略
1. **三段式结构**：
   - **第一段**：描述图表/引出话题（现象描述+论点）。
   - **第二段**：分析原因、影响或辩论双方观点（主体论证）。
   - **第三段**：个人看法/建议/总结升华（结尾）。
2. **高分句式积累**：
   - 图表描述：The chart reveals/illustrates/demonstrates that ...
   - 原因分析：Several factors account for this phenomenon ...
   - 观点表达：From my perspective, ... / In my view, ...
   - 总结升华：In the final analysis, ... / Ultimately, ...
3. **词汇替换**：
   - important → significant, crucial, essential, fundamental
   - think → argue, believe, maintain, contend, hold the view that
   - people → individuals, citizens, the public, the general population
   - good → beneficial, advantageous, favorable, positive
   - bad → harmful, detrimental, adverse, negative
4. **避免常见错误**：
   - 主谓一致
   - 时态一致性（图表题多用现在+过去时；议论文多用现在时）
   - 冠词使用（a/an/the 的缺失与多余）
   - 中式英语表达（逐字翻译）
   - 段落逻辑跳跃（缺少过渡连接词）

### 四、时间管理
- **小作文**：15-20 分钟（5 分钟构思 + 10 分钟写作 + 3 分钟检查）
- **大作文**：30-35 分钟（5 分钟构思 + 20 分钟写作 + 5 分钟检查）
- **总时间**：建议不超过 55 分钟，留给翻译 25-30 分钟。

### 五、复习建议
1. **每周至少写 2 篇作文**（大小各一）。
2. **积累 3-5 套不同主题的万能模板**，但避免机械套用。
3. **准备论据素材库**：教育、环境、科技、文化、社会、心理六大主题，各积累 3-5 个可用的论证角度与具体事例。
4. **研读真题范文**：注意分析范文的结构、论证方式、语言技巧。
5. **坚持修改**：写完后务必重读修改，重点检查语法错误和表达的地道性。
